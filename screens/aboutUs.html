A little bit about us

Our background

Founded in 2024 as an independent game development studio and later became a major retailer of video games and consoles, Mac Industries specializes exclusively in creating and selling high performance, narrative driven video games. We sell a wide variety of consoles and video games from popular companies.

Our mission

To engineer immersive experiences and provide our customers with console optimized gaming experiences that will ensure memorable storytelling and fluid gameplay for hours on end.

Our vision

To become a premier South African console gaming studio recognized inter/nationally for our excellent service, original titles, credibility in the console and video game retailing and dedicated player support.
